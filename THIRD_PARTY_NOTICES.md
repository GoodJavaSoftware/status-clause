## Original project media

The following media was created for Status Claus and is released under the
project's MIT license (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). It is listed here so that every
visual and sound in the extension has a documented source.

- Loading wreath — original vector leaves, berries, and ribbon drawn at runtime
  by `content.js` and animated with CSS.

- `assets/santa-sleigh.png` — original transparent Santa-and-reindeer fly-by
  illustration.
- Dancing elf, candy cane, gingerbread cookie, and peppermint artwork — vector shapes
  drawn at runtime by `treats.js`. No image files are bundled for them.
- Evil snowman — original vector shapes drawn at runtime by `snowman.js`.
  Incoming snowballs are drawn with CSS. No image files are bundled for them.
- Fast-Santa alert beep — three short triangle-wave tones synthesized at
  runtime by `music.js` with the Web Audio API. No recording is bundled.
- Snowball throw and impact sounds — original filtered-noise whoosh and
  noise-plus-tone impact synthesized in `music.js`. No recordings are bundled.
- Sad-trombone ending — an original descending brass phrase synthesized at
  runtime by `music.js` with oscillators, vibrato, and a moving low-pass filter.
  No third-party recording or composition is bundled.

Toys, several falling treats (cupcake, cookie, wrapped candy, chocolate bar,
lollipop, doughnut), the explosion, and the bullseye marker are Unicode emoji
characters. They are rendered by the visitor's operating-system or browser
